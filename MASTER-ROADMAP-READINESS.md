- `implemented` means an identified slice exists in a draft branch; `offline tested` means tests executed in a specified isolated environment; `integrated` requires a fresh checkout against the actual repo and dependencies; `live accepted` requires witnessed real components; `released` requires separate merge/deploy approval. All draft package PRs are **not integrated, not merged, not deployed** unless a later explicit record proves otherwise.
