**Nude base definition:** an underlying adult unclothed character art/rigging asset needed to fit layered clothing, not a default public display or permission to touch intimate regions. Underwear through outerwear is a complete wardrobe scope, not a requirement to publish nude imagery or enable intimate interactions. Unknown or public audience defaults to a covered/canonical outfit. Restrict private visibility with verified adult/session/viewer permission where allowed and prevent leaks through previews, caches, streams and screenshots. Clothing/objects do not imply physical warmth, sensations, robot authority or unrestricted device control.

**Suggested initial objects:** notebook and pen (write/draw only as actual implemented virtual capabilities), mug (grasp/place), multimeter (simulated unless separately authorized trusted data), flashlight (virtual beam), toolkit (open/close and item inventory), chair (sit/stand) and a model-train prop. Object capabilities are explicit and versioned; a narrative or LLM proposal never counts as an executed operation without renderer/tool acknowledgment.

## Position in the existing sequence

1. **Do not displace the current gate:** repair/verify CORE and complete INTERACT's text/headless first slice, then MEM. A0 and offline asset authoring can proceed in parallel if they do not delay those features.
2. Finish SOCIAL's minimum authenticated actor/audience boundaries before Discord DMs. Discord D0–D4 needs only its required PKG-NET connectivity, not a renderer or general internet search.
3. Deliver/reliably test PKG-RUN 24/7 operation after Discord; a fully complete avatar is **not** required for Discord or RUN acceptance.
4. AVATAR A3/A4 require their respective INTERACT/UI/SAFE/MEM prerequisites and verified real render/input. They can be staged independently of the Discord/RUN sequence without silently moving the internet-search gate.
5. Only after Discord **and** real 24/7 acceptance may general internet searching/browsing be introduced as a separately scoped capability. AVATAR never grants it by installing a renderer or an asset pack.

**Remaining decisions to settle by evidence, not guessed into canonical data:** actual 2D/3D format and renderer, approved fine geometry and anatomical detail, art pipeline and licenses, visibility UX, specific undergarment designs, physics fidelity, minimum wardrobe catalogue, prop import policy, save/restore scope, client OS/VRAM budgets and eligible private-mode presentation. Nothing in these plans is proof an avatar exists today.
