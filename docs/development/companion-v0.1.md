## Host acceptance still required

On the actual Windows desktop, enable the renderer and voice controls in
Settings, select the installed voice/default microphone, and verify:

1. Push **Talk**, speak one sentence, and observe its durable user/assistant turn.
2. Interrupt speech with **Talk** and confirm a cancelled TTS receipt.
3. Trigger an ear or tail expression and visually confirm completion alongside a
   `rendered` avatar receipt.
4. Use **Stop gesture** mid-animation and confirm `cancelled`, never `rendered`.
5. Disable/mute voice or disable the renderer and confirm text conversation
   remains available without fabricated output acknowledgement.

These checks require real devices and a visible desktop. They are not claimed by
headless CI.
