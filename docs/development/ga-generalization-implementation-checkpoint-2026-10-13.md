## How to evaluate when authorized

- Original/extended inventory CLI: `python -m sofia.verify.v18_roadmap --repo .`.
- Future independent evaluator-signed transfer evidence CLI:
  `python -m sofia.verify.generalization_transfer --evidence /trusted/reviewed-study.json --evaluator-public-key /trusted/evaluator-public.pem`.
- The public key must originate from an independently trusted evaluator, not be generated by the solver. A signature attests the evaluator's statement, **not** objective truth or a cryptographic guarantee that its timestamp existed in the past. Independently controlled study execution, archival/timestamp controls and external checker evidence remain mandatory.
- Exit code 0 means the signed paired evidence passes the statistical screening only; 1 means no passing transfer signal; 2 means evidence invalid/unreadable. These exit codes never authorize promotion.
