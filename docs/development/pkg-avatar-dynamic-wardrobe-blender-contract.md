# PKG-AVATAR · Dynamic wardrobe, preferences and Blender authoring contract

**2026-09-21. Design + independently testable code candidate in draft PR #7; not merged or deployed.** Owners: PKG-AVATAR supplies garment metadata, slot/outfit plans and approved art; MEM persists provenance and reviewed preferences; REL/CORE may use optional *modeled* appraisal cues; INTERACT mediates gestures; UI/SAFE independently enforce authenticated viewer, privacy and actual rendering; ACT/RUN may schedule optional decisions only after those package gates. Weather retrieval is **not** granted by this feature or PKG-NET's Discord-only stage.

## 1. Body-region fitting and wardrobe slots

- Model **equipment-like slots internally, human-readable outfits externally**. Garments may occupy multiple regions and a layer (`UNDERWEAR`, `BASE`, `MID`, `OUTER`, `ACCESSORY`); duplicate occupied layer+slot is rejected. Existing coarse torso/pelvis/legs/hands/head/ears/tail remain available; optional shoulders/upper arms/forearms/wrists/fingers/waist/back/thighs/calves/ankles/hair and left/right hands, forearms, wrists, feet support gauntlets, gloves and accessories. These are *wardrobe authoring slots*, **not** a verified versioned canonical anatomy/hit-test map. Audit actual `src/sofia/data/avatar.json` and map them before A1/A3.
- Example: a pair of leather gauntlets uses separate `left_forearm`+`left_wrist` and `right_forearm`+`right_wrist` accessories. A coat/hood claiming tail/ear slots needs clearance metadata; actual mesh clearance, fit and skin coverage require inspected geometry and real renderer tests.
- Outfit plans contain reviewed item IDs, compatible activities, seasons, optional weather, lounge/private flags and style tags. The base Wardrobe validates conflicts and torso/pelvis coverage; an `asset_ref` string does **not** prove artwork exists.

## 2. Routine and wardrobe context

- A *trusted host*, not the LLM, supplies offset-aware **local time**, calendar cadence, season based on actual location/hemisphere and optional source/timestamped weather observation. An unknown, future or >6-hour-old observation is ignored. No implicit web search, geography inference or weather service is installed. The first release can operate on clock, selected season and approved manual weather evidence only; weather APIs are a **later, separately authorized** feature after Discord + verified 24/7.
- Context influences candidate ranking for ordinary conversation, engineering, virtual lab, relaxing, sleep or formal activities. Late local evening or early morning during conversation/relaxing/sleep makes covered lounge wear (e.g., **oversized T-shirt and sweatpants**) plausible; an ongoing late-night engineering task is not forcibly interrupted to change clothing. Season and source-backed weather, practical suitability, personal taste and recent *verified worn* evidence all matter.
- Daily, ISO-weekly and monthly rotation are **options**, not a running background job. A previous renderer-confirmed choice may persist during a cadence period while still appropriate; changed weather/activity can justify a new *proposal*. No recorded renderer acknowledgement means no claim that the outfit was worn. Stale/future/duplicate receipts cannot authorize a change.
- The deterministic scorer is an offline starting heuristic; no fixed numerical formula defines Sofía's personality. Review variety, seasonal carryover, privacy, genuine comfort of *visual style* and live naturalness before deploying an autonomous selector. A familiar signature look can remain a favorite rather than being rotated away compulsively.

## 3. Sparks and Sofía preferences

- Keep separate **Sparks feedback** and **Sofía's modeled taste candidates**, linked to source records. Accept like/love/dislike/hate for an outfit, a single item/slot garment, or a sorted combination (e.g., gauntlets with jacket). Feedback about one outfit does not automatically generalize to an unrelated region, person or action.
- An LLM utterance alone cannot silently become a durable favorite, verified emotion or permission. Candidate preferences need source and **reviewed** status before this offline scorer uses them; contradictory revisions must be reconciled in MEM, not silently applied in arbitrary list order. Correct/retract/delete must propagate to wardrobe projections.
- Sofía's reviewed modeled taste has a distinct, somewhat stronger default contribution than Sparks's preference in the prototype; it is **not** an immutable personality trait or an override of your safety/privacy settings. Your likes can influence choices while she still retains a consistent visual style. Revisit weightings using non-repetitive live evaluation and explicit user feedback. Never treat a dislike as evidence of actual pain or a body sensation.

## 4. Chosen vs unintended clothing and modeled emotion

- A deliberate private change may offer contextual expressive cues such as comfort, confidence or *possible* excitement. An unintended underdressed state may offer surprise, self-consciousness, possible embarrassment or a wish to change. These are **optional candidates** for REL/CORE, not guaranteed reactions, real subjective feelings, sexual arousal, or consent.
- Context matters: audience, intent, current task, authentic relationship evidence, personal taste, privacy and corrections. A chosen outfit is not necessarily enjoyable; an accidental change is not automatically shameful. Avoid rigid `exposure -> emotion` meters, needy dialogue, automatic flirtation or manipulative dependence.
- The routine's **automatic selection never proposes uncovered/private-only clothing**, even in private. Uncovered or unintended changes require covered-recovery handling and **never authorize publishing**. Explicit, permitted private previews remain subject to the existing `PreviewRequest` guard and independently verified adult/session/owner/current opt-in and external stop, not a model claim. Reduced clothing is never interaction consent. Cross-client previews, thumbnails, crash fallbacks, caches and screenshots require separate privacy review.

## 5. Blender A0/A1/A2 and future Sofía-authored outfits

- **Start in Blender with an A0 blockout**, not a final anatomy or a nude asset. `tools/avatar/blender_blockout.py` creates a fully dressed, clearly labeled placeholder with crimson hair, violet ears/tail, large T-shirt, sweatpants, gauntlet proxies and empty slot anchors. Its proportions are unapproved placeholders. It does not rig or weight paint, fit cloth, produce licensed final meshes, generate animations or satisfy an A1 unclothed adult authoring base. It does not overwrite existing scenes or outputs and requires an explicit save path for CLI export.
- **Next supervised A1:** review canonical appearance/age presentation, art style, measurements, source/license, topology, skeleton, ear/tail bones, mesh body-region map, UVs and privacy-safe previews. **A2:** approved shirt/pants/engineer jacket/gauntlet outfit + oversized T-shirt/sweatpants lounge set, underlayers, layer fit, socks/boots and private presentation. Inspect clipping in movement, poses and changing states, then real renderer evidence at A3. Reuse one consistent rig/slots instead of modeling a new character per outfit.
- **Later Sofía outfit design:** begin with proposed combinations of **approved existing assets and parameters**, stored as reviewable manifests. A human-approved, bounded Blender adapter may generate a *sandboxed draft* garment or variation, compare asset/license and script/diff, render a preview in an isolated worker and await review before promotion. No free-form generated Python executed in the production desktop; arbitrary Blender scripts can run with host permissions. No unrestricted filesystem, subprocess, internet, unsafe import, protected-file editing, physical hardware or self-authorized publishing. DEV/SAFE control executor/approval, AVATAR owns art, VERIFY checks outputs and rollback.
