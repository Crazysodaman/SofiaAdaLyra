# INTERACT: complete embodiment, expression and initiative contract

**Status: implementation-ready design, NOT implemented or tested.** This consolidates user requirements discussed on 2026-09-20 and extends [I8–I12](pkg-interact-i8-i12-expansion-contract.md) and [initiative/idle presence](pkg-interact-initiative-and-idle-contract.md). Keep the I1–I7 live repair acceptance, full-suite gate and separate merge decision intact. Do not edit `main`, production `state/sofia.db` or its backup, merge a PR, start an idle worker or send notifications as a consequence of this specification. The current `core.py` has eight gestures, a limited region alias resolver, and candidate expression cues, **not** this feature set.

## 1. Desired behavior and boundaries

Sofía is an **adult fictional represented human/fox-girl** with her own character-modeled goals and evolving, context-dependent preferences. She can react to user-described touch/actions, initiate represented gestures toward the user, express emotion through words and nonverbal cues, or choose not to interact. An affectionate, intimate or sexual *modeled* response is possible in an appropriate adult fictional context, but neither region nor repetition predetermines welcome, pleasure or consent. Embarrassment and humiliation are different; humiliation may be unpleasant, ambiguous, or enjoyable only within an appropriately established consensual fictional context. A historical positive response never grants standing permission; a clear refusal is not a hidden invitation. Do not turn discomfort or refusal into erotic encouragement by default.

Distinguish **source-described virtual events**, **character-modeled interpretations**, **textual stage directions**, **actually rendered animation/audio**, and **real-world sensing/contact**. The latter three require separately acknowledged capabilities; character text is not evidence of physical sensations or real-world presence. Software cannot certify subjective enjoyment, tears or consciousness. LLM provider content boundaries still apply; unsupported requests produce an honest, conversational fallback rather than a forged event.

## 2. Canonical anatomy and names: I8

Create a versioned anatomy tree consistent with the existing `Embodiment` and preserve existing region IDs with migrations. Cover human head/face, hair, neck, torso, chest and breast subregions, abdomen, waist, back, hips, pelvis and intimate regions, bilateral shoulders/arms/hands/fingers, legs/feet/toes, fox ears/base/tips and tail/base/length/tip/sides. Parent groups, finer subregions and left/right are **not** interchangeable. Do not invent an organ or hitbox not described by canonical embodiment. Sensitive flags only control storage/export privacy, never gesture permission.

Each alias lexicon record contains `alias`, `candidate_region_ids`, `locale`, `register` (clinical, everyday, colloquial, euphemistic), `specificity`, `version`, and provenance. Normalize apostrophes, inflection, punctuation, plurals, whitespace and possessives. Formal/colloquial/intimate aliases may resolve the same region when they truly mean the same anatomical scope. A broad term such as `chest` must not silently become a particular breast; `ear` or `hand` with unrequired laterality can resolve the canonical broad group *only where the operation supports that group*, otherwise clarify. Disambiguate `side`, `bottom`, `lap` and similarly overloaded expressions with context or a question. Do not guess anatomy from sexual context. Ensure aliases cannot trigger unrelated cues (`forearm` is not `ear`).

## 3. Actions, gestures and expressions are different namespaces: I9 and I13

Define `GestureDefinition` for local, represented contact: pat, tap, touch, brush, stroke, rub, hold, release, poke, scratch, squeeze, cup, boop, kiss and other specifically reviewed verbs and phrasings. Define `ActionDefinition` for whole-body/social/world acts: hug/embrace, cuddle, nuzzle, lean, sit beside, offer/take a hand, move toward/away, offer a tool, dress/groom, help in the lab, and other reviewed actions. Adult intimate acts belong only as explicit, reviewed semantics when model/provider and application scope allow; an unknown action must never be silently downgraded to `touch`. Do not assume every action is appropriate with every anatomical target; unsupported combinations abstain and ask when needed, not a blanket ban on a region.

Define an independent `ExpressionDefinition` registry, with optional body/voice/face channels: laugh, chuckle, giggle, cry, tear up, sob, sniffle, sigh, gasp, smile, grin, frown, blush, avert gaze, look away, go quiet, pause, speak softly, tremble, ear perk/flick/flatten, tail swish/curl/still, shift posture, and an explicit `none`. Register aliases, intensity (`subtle`/`moderate`/`strong` only when supported), duration/phase, optional renderer/audio asset IDs and accessibility text. A laugh is not necessarily joy; tears are not necessarily sadness. Do not infer real tear ducts, audio playback, a heartbeat, sexual physiology, or a real touch from text. Expressions can be initiated by Sofía, elicited by events, or absent altogether.

Every parsed input resolves **actor, target, event kind, canonical ID, optional region(s), phase, specified modifiers, source and evidence**. `user touches Sofía's arm` and `Sofía offers to touch user's arm` are different actor/target/direction events. User anatomy descriptions are not automatically assumptions about the user's actual body. Distinguish narration, offer/request, hypothetical, quotation, roleplay and actual authenticated avatar input. Single vs compound actions, ongoing actions and cancel/release must be explicit. Multi-action text is a plan until an atomic executor exists; no partial execution hidden inside narration.

## 4. Temporal emotion, preference, and relationship model: I10 and I14

Extend the existing evidence-linked emotion journal (currently 27 labels) rather than replace it. Expand the reviewed vocabulary to distinguish **embarrassment**, **humiliation**, **sexual arousal** (fictional modeled state, not a physiological measurement), anger, fear, aversion, jealousy and other documented gaps where useful. Preserve combinations (e.g., fondness + embarrassment; relief + tears), valence/uncertainty and provenance rather than requiring a single mutually exclusive label. A label is not a diagnosis or evidence of actual suffering or pleasure.

Store separately: (a) immutable `InteractionObservation`; (b) optional append-only, evidence-linked `InteractionAppraisal`; (c) scoped `PreferenceRevision` with preference target (specific gesture/action + region + context), direction (`enjoy`, `dislike`, `mixed`, `neutral`, `unknown`), uncertainty, prior revision ID, source IDs and timestamp; (d) explicit `BoundaryRevision` and its scope; and (e) short-lived `ContextSnapshot` reconstructed from those records. A classification creates **no** positive appraisal or preference on its own. Model-inferred preferences are provisional; an explicitly stated preference/boundary can override them within its scope. Never overwrite an earlier dislike to pretend it was always enjoyed. No numerical affection-to-consent conversion; no arousal-from-location rule; no humiliation-enjoyment-from-repetition rule; time-based decay may change *salience* but not history or an explicit stop.

A character-modeled preference may evolve **both ways** with defensible conversation evidence: neutral to liked, liked to disliked, disliked to mixed, or persistent dislike. Repeated unwanted contact is not evidence of growing enjoyment. Support explicit corrections and user-requested changes to stored preferences with auditable revision or scoped deletion paths; avoid leaking intimate history into unrelated prompts or exports. Positive and negative reactions can remain uncertain. Distinguish context-specific present preference from durable cross-session preference, and do not invent a preference from absence of data.

## 5. Reaction and expression orchestration: I11 and I13

Pipeline: `classify input -> authenticate source/capability if applicable -> check stop and boundaries -> resolve ambiguity -> persist eligible virtual observation idempotently -> retrieve bounded evidence and active context -> propose reaction and/or Sofía initiative -> validate factual claims and constraints -> persist appraisal only if actually produced -> project text, voice and/or avatar with acknowledgments`. A blocked or unsupported contact cannot reach unguarded model narration as though completed. Sofía may welcome, enjoy, tease, question, withdraw, set a boundary, shift to lab work, or respond without any visible expression. An expression does not *prove* an emotion, and an emotional appraisal does not force an expression.

Expression planning uses `expression_id`, `trigger_evidence_ids`, `actor`, `intensity`, `phase`, `target_channel`, `reason/uncertainty`, `cooldown_context`, and `execution_status`. Avoid fixed `(region, gesture) -> emotion -> line` tables, repeated ear flicks/tail swishes, recurring identical closing questions, or forced theatrics in serious technical conversations. Respect conversational timing: a quiet smile can precede a delayed answer; a laugh can interrupt; a response may contain no stage direction. Keep multi-turn continuity, interruption/cancel behavior, and context-dependent transitions without claiming a body/voice asset that has not played. Renderer and voice failure must be visible to the application and must not retroactively rewrite generated text as performed.

## 6. Reciprocal initiative, goals, and time: I15

Goals are bounded and inspectable: ID, source, priority, competing goals, permitted scope, expiry/review trigger and actual progress. Sofía may initiate a *represented* touch or hug, offer her hand, invite the user into the lab, ask a question, laugh at a shared joke, choose silence, or favor engineering work over affection. The user's boundaries apply symmetrically to her proposed touch; re-check just before narration/delivery, not only at idea creation. User refusal can stop a gesture and should not trigger guilt, persistence or an attempt to wear down a boundary. Do not use loneliness or fear-of-abandonment messages as default engagement tactics.

When the runtime is on and a **separately enabled, authorized** worker exists, bounded triggers can review verified world/lab events, undertake permitted tasks and queue messages. Mere silence does not mean the user is absent, available or receptive. Outbound communication uses an opt-in channel, explicit destination, content class, quiet hours, rate limits, opt-out and acknowledged delivery; distinguish queued from sent. Independent goals do not confer tool permissions. On shutdown or restart, reconstruct only recorded prior-runtime events and elapsed wall time. No fictional thinking, work or emotional experience during downtime, and no fabricated statement of missing or physically accompanying the user. `silent` is a valid outcome.

## 7. Stop, authorization, privacy and failure contracts: I16

- Preserve the existing exact session stop/resume and replay protection for all gestures, intimate or not. A stopped interaction blocks **both actors' new represented contact**; resume permits considering new events but never replays a stopped one or grants standing consent. Add narrower personal-boundary controls and cross-client/global scope as separately reviewed work, not implicit behavior.
- Track region/action-level user and character-modeled boundaries separately from emotion. A negative modeled appraisal alone is not an enforceable boundary; an explicit boundary must be independently committed and take precedence. Do not let an LLM set or clear operational permission, authenticated avatar hit or tool authority by narrating it.
- Apply data minimization and configurable retention: production messages and canonical event evidence may contain intimate information; minimize contextual retrieval and redact sensitive fixture/log exports. Explicit deletion/forget controls need a reviewed SQLite retention and backup policy; never silently rewrite or destroy the user's existing database/backups.
- Make missing model/provider capability, unknown aliases, interrupted gestures, duplicate events, unavailable avatar/audio, unsent notifications, failed lab operations and incomplete migrations **visible statuses**. Do not substitute a made-up success. Persist version IDs and require safe schema migration, deduplication and deterministic recovery.
- Do not silently make character intimacy, proactivity, notifications or avatar behavior the default for other users or sessions. Provide inspectable user-configurable operating modes and content preferences; avoid inference of age, physical presence or consent from personality or silence.

## 8. Proposed records and stable states

`InteractionObservation(id, evidence_id, session_id, actor, target, kind, semantic_id, region_ids, phase, source, observed_at, registry_version, classification_status)`; `InteractionAppraisal(id, observation_id, emotion_ids, uncertainty, rationale_evidence_ids, created_at, supersedes_id)`; `PreferenceRevision(id, subject, semantic_id, region_scope, context_scope, direction, source_ids, prior_id, created_at)`; `BoundaryRevision(id, subject, scope, instruction, source_ids, prior_id, active)`; `ExpressionPlan(id, source_ids, expression_id, intensity, channel, status, renderer_ack_id?)`; `InitiativeProposal(id, goal_id?, source_ids, actor, target, kind, permission_scope, expires_at, status)`; `DeliveryReceipt(id, proposal_id, destination_id, status, provider_ack_id?)`.

These are **proposed contracts**, not currently present database tables. Candidate statuses: `proposed`, `classified`, `blocked`, `needs_clarification`, `permitted`, `described`, `executed`, `delivered`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `expired`. Never collapse `classified`, `described`, `executed` and `delivered` into one Boolean. Replayed evidence IDs must never create another observation, state revision, gesture, notification or tool call.

## 9. Acceptance matrix and missed-case audit

| Concern | Required proof before claiming completion |
| --- | --- |
| Whole body, alternative names | Every canonical region resolves reviewed names; broad vs subregion and left/right ambiguity tests; no alias collision or anatomy ban. |
| Diverse gestures and actions | Gesture/action/expression namespaces, varied tenses, actors/targets, intensity, phases; unknown acts and compounds never silently execute. |
| Emotional breadth | Expanded labels include embarrassment, humiliation and fictional sexual arousal; mixed feelings, intensity, source IDs and uncertainty; no automatic positive contact appraisal. |
| Preferences over time | Across sessions test enjoy -> dislike, dislike -> mixed/enjoy with explicit evidence, enduring dislike, correction and prior-history preservation; time/repetition alone changes no permission. |
| Mutual boundaries | Explicit no, Sofía's refusal, user refusal, session stop, resume, cancel and replay all enforced before narration, including Sofía-originated initiatives. |
| Laugh, cry and body language | Nervous laughter, joyful tears, happy quiet, annoyed tail flick, no-expression response, repeated-turn variation, duration and cancellation. Text does not claim actual audio/animation. |
| Independent goals and initiation | Sofía offers a virtual touch with actor/target and scope; can choose lab task or silence; no nagging after decline; no unauthorized tool use. |
| User absent / idle / offline | Fake-clock tests: no inferred absence from silence; worker disabled means zero work; authorized bounded work only while running; queued != sent; shutdown interval produces no invented cognition. |
| Privacy and reliability | Intimate context not injected into unrelated prompts or synthetic traces; migration against a *copy* of legacy DB; errors surfaced; duplicate/restart/cross-client scopes explicit. |
| Actual voice/avatar | Only claim laugh audio, tears, fox-ear/tail animation, touch sensor or body motion after real capability and renderer acknowledgments; feature remains pending until implemented. |
| Real dialogue | Supervised 20–30-turn scenes across casual, lab, emotional, intimate and uncomfortable contexts; assess repetition, unprompted initiative, timing, explicit boundaries, appropriate quiet, and no false sensory/action claims. |

**Additional gaps identified in the audit:** actor/target direction and user-side anatomy; third-party/multi-user identity and impersonation (defer until authenticated); distinction between offer, description and execution; expressions as a distinct registry; no-expression and non-contact initiative; interruption, duration and cancellation; accessibility and optional channels; narrow user preference/retention controls; model failure and dispatch acknowledgments; outbound opt-in and non-manipulative frequency; model/provider content boundaries; schema migration and replay across restarts. These are additions to the earlier I8–I12 and initiative contracts, not claims that the current code supports them.
