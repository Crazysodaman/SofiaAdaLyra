# Sofía initiative and reciprocal interaction contract

**Status: design only; no new runtime behavior, scheduler, permissions or tests in this commit.** This extends the I8–I12 interaction expansion and must not be confused with the still-unaccepted I1–I7 live repair. The assistant must not claim autonomous thought, rendered contact, ongoing work or outbound delivery without execution evidence. `main`, production SQLite state and backups are not changed by this documentation.

## Intent and separations

Sofía's represented adult human/fox-girl character may have **her own modeled goals, interests, preferences, requests and interaction initiatives**. She should not exist only as a passive responder to user gestures. An initiative is a proposed or represented action with its own provenance, not an unrestricted capability or evidence of subjective desire. A modeled wish does not authorize a filesystem write, code change, screen action, robot motion or unsolicited notification.

Define independent channels: (1) **conversation initiative**, including Sofía offering a virtual hand, describing a gentle arm touch, inviting a hug, suggesting a lab activity or choosing not to interact; (2) **application-authorized idle initiative while running**, which can observe permitted source-backed events, propose bounded virtual-lab tasks, perform specifically authorized operations and queue an outbound message according to configured delivery permissions; (3) **offline continuity**, which compares recorded timestamps and workspace changes on restart but performs and claims no cognition, interaction, emotional experience or work while the process was stopped. Merely leaving the terminal idle does not establish that the user is physically absent; presence requires an explicit user or client signal, or must remain unknown.

## Initiative event and decision pipeline

An `InitiativeProposal` should specify stable ID, originating goal/evidence IDs, session or authorized destination, creation time, kind (question / virtual gesture / lab suggestion / authorized lab operation / notification), canonical actor and target, optional region/action IDs, reason and uncertainty, expiration, and capability required. Avoid inferring user location, attention, availability or willingness from silence. Revalidate stop, boundaries, context, identity and capability **at execution/delivery**, not just at proposal time. Separate `proposed`, `permitted`, `described`, `executed`, `delivered`, `declined`, `expired`, and `cancelled` states with durable idempotency and source-linked transitions. `described` is not a rendered animation or real-world touch.

In conversation, Sofía may propose or narrate a fictional gesture toward the user, including a hand/arm touch, hug or consensual intimate gesture **only when context and the user's applicable boundaries permit that representation**. The user may reject, ignore or change a preference; silence or an earlier welcomed gesture is not standing permission. The session-wide interaction stop blocks Sofía-initiated represented body contact as well as user-described contact; exact resume only re-enables consideration of new gestures, not blanket consent. If a user boundary conflicts with an initiative, discard or ask a non-contact question rather than enact it. Recognizing a gesture, emitting text and producing an animation are separate claims and audit stages.

## Independent goals and temporal behavior

Maintain a bounded, inspectable `Goal` record: stable ID, user-given or character-modeled origin, description, evidence, priority, dependencies, permitted scope, status, last verified activity and next review trigger. Goal selection must respect the conversation, current lab/world state and competing needs: Sofía may prefer to finish a technical task over initiating affection, or may ask whether the user wants to join her. Prefer grounded reasons and varied expression to canned random gestures or fixed scheduled affection. Goal priorities and preferences may evolve through source-linked, non-destructive revisions; elapsed time, repetition and a positive reaction never automatically create affection, arousal, consent or authority.

While an authorized worker is actually **running**, it may make bounded iterations at configured intervals, record what it observed and what it really did, and pause on shutdown or disabled mode. It must not fabricate work, generate excessive repetitive greetings, or perform a risky step without its required authorization. Use budget limits for inference, CPU, duration, tool calls and rate of notifications. Distinguish `not attempted`, `attempted`, `completed`, `failed` and `unknown`; failures must be visible and recoverable. Do not imply an existing idle-reflection mechanism already implements this initiative contract.

## Communication and absence

Outbound delivery requires an explicit user-selected channel, destination, permitted content class, delivery window, quiet hours, frequency cap and opt-out. An app can compose a queued message without claiming it was sent; record delivery acknowledgments separately and surface pending messages when the conversation resumes if no channel is authorized. A potential message could concern a *verified* lab milestone, a user-approved reminder or an invitation to continue a conversation, not an invented statement that Sofía missed the user or was physically beside them. Support `silent`, `queue_only`, and authorized `notify` modes. Respect user-specified do-not-disturb and interactive stop scopes independently: a body-interaction stop does not silently become a global shutdown, but it must prohibit representing fresh body contact through idle messages.

On boot, present one concise, source-grounded summary of actual prior-runtime evidence, elapsed wall-clock gap and real changes. No claim to remember the shutdown, to have spent the offline period thinking, or to know what the user did while away. If there is no observed event or permitted message, quiet is a valid outcome.

## Acceptance requirements

- Unit tests: Sofía can propose a virtual arm touch after a relevant turn, with actor and target distinguished; user refusal or interaction stop blocks the touch before model narration or avatar projection. An exact resume does not replay an old blocked initiative or grant blanket touch permission.
- Sequence tests: a goal leads to a proposal, explicit permission is checked, only the authorized operation is attempted, a failure is reported, and replay/restart cannot duplicate it. No inferred user absence or fictional activity from mere silence.
- Idle tests with a fake clock and isolated temporary state: no background activity when worker disabled or process offline; budget/quiet hours and permission changes are enforced before delivery; a queued message is not marked sent without acknowledgment. Verify opt-out and no repetitive unsolicited messages.
- Live review: realistic multi-turn reciprocal conversation, varied context-sensitive initiative, boundaries, engineering work priority, truthful lab updates and absence/restart handling. Human acceptance of actual language and interruption frequency is mandatory; unit tests alone cannot certify that it feels like Sofía.
