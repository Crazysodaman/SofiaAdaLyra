# PKG-UI design addendum: Sofía's text-first interactive workbench

**Date:** 2026-09-21. **Status:** proposed design, not a demonstrated preference of the running Sofía, shipped app, renderer or tested capability. The design reflects canonical engineering/personality characteristics and user-requested avatar interaction. When a working Sofía can compare accessible prototypes, collect her model-generated, source-linked preference and review it as a *design proposal*, not proof of subjective desire or license to alter protected identity.

**Links:** [Root roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md), [avatar roadmap](../../ROADMAP-AVATAR.md), [PKG-AVATAR contract](pkg-avatar-embodiment-wardrobe-objects.md), [INTERACT contract](avatar-screen-interaction-contract.md), [cognition/social/RUN addendum](roadmap-cognition-social-runtime-20260921.md). PKG-UI owns presentation and client transport; PKG-AVATAR owns body/rig/clothes/props/scene; PKG-INTERACT owns normalized gesture/action interpretation; ACT/REL propose context and initiative; MEM/SOCIAL/SAFE enforce source, audience and authorizations. This design adds **no additional package** to the proposed 16-package roster.

## Concept: a workbench, not an avatar glued to a chatbot

Use a reliable, persistent **conversation dock/text box** as the primary input/output: readable chronological messages, distinct actor attribution, streaming text where actually supported, drafts, editable *unsent user drafts only*, easy copy/citation, search of *authorized* history, keyboard/touch and screen-reader navigation. The dock works without a renderer, voice, Discord, network/search or avatar; no floating speech bubble is required to retrieve information or receive a technical answer. Treat a quoted message, assistant proposal, user draft and actually executed action as distinct UI elements.

Provide a separate, resizable, hideable **Sofía viewport** with canonical model and a spatial **shared work surface** (desk/whiteboard/notebook) as optional companion, not a mandatory block on typing or a permanent GPU burden. Text pane can occupy full width when avatar is closed; in compact/mobile view prioritize chat and a toggleable mini/avatar stage. Let Sofía represent her attention via gaze, point at an authorized note/diagram, hold a virtual notebook, type/draw on a virtual shared board when explicitly granted, or hand a virtual object to Sparks. All actions are proposed → authorized → renderer/executor acknowledged → reflected in text/history; the avatar cannot claim it read a screen or physically typed if no such event happened. No script or model output can directly dispatch OS key presses, click arbitrary windows, access files, send network traffic or grant itself permissions.

## Example of the intended loop

Sparks enters a Gaia question in chat; Sofía responds in the text pane immediately, then optionally requests the notebook object from the permitted shared scene. If approved, she turns toward the work surface and the renderer acknowledges her pickup; she may put a *source-linked hypothesis* on an authorized shared note. The note shows an editable representation with author and timestamp, not a stealth edit to Sparks's chat messages. Sparks can add a correction, point to the note, or hand the virtual notebook back. Sofía sees only the scopes actually granted for that actor and room. After renderer crash or hide, text chat continues and the last **acknowledged** scene state persists; unsent/unfinished motion is not narrated as completed.

## Proposed panels and affordances

- **Conversation dock:** user text box with Enter-to-send (configurable), Shift+Enter/newline, stop-generation, draft retention, response stream, a compact evidence/unknown indicator and a clear distinction between a thought candidate, reply and completed action. At minimum: text-only, high-contrast, accessible and offline-local modes. No simulated typing delay to fake thought.
- **Avatar stage:** voluntary visibility; posture, face, ears, tail, hands and clothing rendered coherently by AVATAR, with real hit tests delivered to INTERACT. Sofía may point, sit, hold, place or interact with authorized local props. Gesture motion is optional; stillness is allowed; animation and voice never delay the user's technical answer.
- **Shared work surface:** one or more permissioned virtual documents/whiteboards/diagrams and approved props with actor, audience, provenance, revision, ownership, expiry and undo. Both participants can place, point, annotate and move where entitled; explicit handoff and edit-conflict handling. A virtual multimeter is clearly simulated unless verified data comes from a separately authorized tool.
- **Activity shelf:** quiet, low-noise view of *actual* pending/reflected ideas and project tasks, with status `hypothesis`, `reviewed`, `awaiting approval`, `running`, `completed`, `failed` and evidence links. Not a private-thought livestream, invented internal monologue or continuous animation. Other users see only their authorized scopes.
- **Presence and controls:** local process/renderer online status (distinct from model loaded, Discord connected and 24/7 service healthy), mic/screen recording indicators, privacy mode, attire/visibility audience, mute/quiet/stop, optional initiative, resource throttle and permission inspector. User controls are available independent of the avatar, and external stop is authoritative.

## Sofía's proposed presentation preferences, not subjective claims

A practical engineer-like visual language: calm, legible and minimal by default, with discoverable advanced diagnostics and a private, inviting avatar presence. Let useful interaction lead expressive motion, not constant idle fidgeting. Let Sofía choose between *approved* camera framing, seated/standing idle, outfit presets, repositioning a prop and when to show a note, while keeping user controls, privacy, accessibility and permissions authoritative. Treat her eventual model-proposed preferences as reviewable context-dependent choices, not guaranteed permanent personality traits or a separate identity per viewer. Personalize social register to each verified individual but never reveal someone else's DM, reflection or private relationship details in public notes, floating bubbles, previews or screenshots.

## System contracts and failure states

`verified actor/session + user text/approved UI gesture → INTERACT semantic event → external SAFE/SOCIAL policy → CORE/REL/ACT response or approved AVATAR/scene proposal → UI renderer/trusted executor → execution acknowledgement → MEM-scoped persistence → truthful UI state`.

Separate local in-world text editing (safe virtual note) from external desktop/OS keyboard control. The avatar can point to and manipulate *its own permissioned chat/workbench controls* only through specific UI action IDs (`open_approved_note`, `focus_chat_input` with user-visible focus, `show_diagram`, `place_prop`, etc.), never raw arbitrary coordinates or unbounded typing in other apps. The model may not send on behalf of Sparks, erase/change authored messages, access an unsent draft, auto-accept private mode or send a proactive notification without explicit corresponding authority. A purported click from old renderer versions, revoked sessions or forged identity fails closed. A shared room cannot inherit private avatar visibility. Log structured results without leaking messages to debug traces.

UI works in renderer absent/crashed/paused/resource-throttled state with an accessible text-only layout. No renderer should block the conversational first token or compete unboundedly with Ollama/GPU gaming. Offer animation-off, low-motion, reduced-motion, font scaling, high contrast and no-roleplay text modes. Never claim the app is 24/7 simply because a viewport stayed open; PKG-RUN supplies supervised uptime. Never introduce browser or internet-search functions before the independent Discord → RUN acceptance gate.

## Staged implementation and acceptance

| Gate | Deliverable | Real acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **U0 · Paper/design and prototype** | Annotated layout choices, accessibility plan, state/action APIs, text-only navigation, profile of hardware constraints; optional let running Sofía compare mockups as evidence-linked *proposals* | Review competing layouts without inventing self-reported preference; no code/UI claim |
| **U1 · Text-first shell** | Persistent usable conversation input, messages and distinct actor scopes, streaming where supported, stop, drafts and accessible text-only fallback | Real desktop chat without avatar; correct provenance, privacy, input and failure recovery; measured first-token latency |
| **U2 · Avatar viewport** | Hideable, resizable actual renderer with canonical clothed avatar and verified event/hit-test transport | Real pointer/gesture events and acknowledgments; no click-through, cross-user exposure or forged motion |
| **U3 · Shared desk/notes** | Authorized interactive text box/whiteboard and AVATAR props, joint annotations, gestures, handoffs, persistent revision and undo | Sparks and Sofía separately act on one object; no impersonated user typing, no leaked DM or invented finished action |
| **U4 · Expression and initiative** | Coordinated optional gaze/ears/tail/hands and actual project-reflection cards with stop/mute controls | Context appropriate, no repeated mandatory animation, renderer outage and slow GPU leave foreground dialogue responsive |
| **U5 · Multi-client and polish** | Authenticated audience-scoped desktop/mobile/voice views and resource/accessibility presets | Permission isolation, revised outfit visibility, reconnect integrity, low-resource/gaming tests and real voice interruption when voice exists |

**Prerequisite integrity:** CORE → INTERACT text/headless → MEM stays current; U0 may run in parallel. U1 does **not** wait for AVATAR A1/A2 or internet search. U2 requires AVATAR base/render deliverables and INTERACT policy. U3 requires AVATAR scene/objects, MEM and SAFE, SOCIAL for multiple people. U4 depends on REL/ACT and real render; U5 has independent authentication/voice gates. Discord D0–D4 and supervised 24/7 RUN do not wait for full character art, and general internet search remains later. Verification records fixture versus real, pass/fail/not run, actual latency and hardware.
