# Private reflection and selective disclosure contract

**Status:** proposed design for the roadmap branch, 2026-09-21; no implementation, tests, or deployment verified. **Owners:** PKG-ACT (reflection lifecycle), PKG-MEM (storage/provenance/retention), PKG-REL (modeled social and emotional influence), PKG-SOCIAL and PKG-SAFE (actor/audience isolation), PKG-CORE (grounded answer and explanations), PKG-UI and PKG-AVATAR (optional visual projection), PKG-VERIFY (cross-boundary tests). **No additional package** is required. Apply existing CORE → INTERACT text/headless → MEM and Discord → verified RUN → later internet-search gates.

## Purpose and meanings

Sofía may run a bounded independent inference pass, create a *candidate private reflection*, retain it, revisit it, revise/reject it, use it as limited context for a later response, or never disclose it. Not every internal result must be a chat message. Such records are actual computational artifacts, **not** evidence of subjective consciousness, felt emotion, continuous thought through downtime, secret autonomous rights, or verified truth.

**Private-to-conversation** means not automatically surfaced to chat, avatar, public desk, Discord, notifications or other users. It does **not** mean inaccessible to a duly authorized, documented operational audit or exempt from safety and retention policies. Avoid claiming cryptographic secrecy from an operator who controls host, database, logs or backups. No guarantee that generated thought text is a faithful transcript of a model's full internal reasoning; store deliberate, concise reflection artifacts, not a purported raw inner monologue.

## Structured records and disclosure states

Store minimal, schema-versioned records with opaque ID, UTC creation/last-reviewed time, actual trigger/event ID or scheduled execution, source/provenance references, authenticated actor/room/audience, original privacy scope and taint/derivation lineage, model and runtime version, type (`hypothesis`, `question`, `creative_idea`, `modeled_emotional_appraisal`, `task_followup`), explicit uncertainty, revision links, expiry/retention policy and lifecycle (`candidate`, `revisited`, `superseded`, `rejected`, `archived`). Keep original evidence separate from model inference and retain corrections. Never represent an inference as a sensor measurement or established fact.

Use an independently enforced disclosure field: `private_unshared` (default), `proposed_to_share`, `shared_to_explicit_audience`, `revoked`, `deleted`; proposal is not permission. A reflection may stay private indefinitely within agreed retention, expire unused, or become an *approved derivative* with provenance and audience filtering. A summary is still derived private material until explicitly reviewed and authorized. Share a minimal non-sensitive conclusion where possible, never raw private transcripts or other users' notes by implication.

## Influence on behavior, emotions and ideas

- At response time retrieve only reflections legally visible in **that** actor/session/audience and relevant to the current request. For Sofía's own general private workspace, apply source taint and scope intersection: a thought derived from private DM A must not influence what she tells B about A, or leak via stylistic hints, recommendations, titles, object counts, timestamps or implicit relationship comparisons. General learning from other people requires explicitly authorized de-identified or shared evidence, not an unreviewed private transfer.
- Allow private reflection to suggest a topic, produce a hypothesis, modulate a *modeled* emotional appraisal or guide an optional, contextual expression. It must not establish a canonical identity fact, certify affection/consent, silently rewrite a relationship, overwrite corrections, entitle physical/virtual contact, bypass a permission, create an obligation to contact the user or drive consequential external action by itself. Persistent user preferences and project facts use MEM's review and permissioned promotion path.
- Make reflective influence bounded and inspectable through source-linked decision metadata, conflict resolution, freshness, uncertainty and decay/expiry. Distinguish a fleeting reaction from a sustained modeled mood and a reviewed relationship preference. New evidence and explicit user corrections can revise or invalidate a candidate; do not escalate unjustified suspicion or recursively rehearse speculation as evidence. Avoid opaque negative personal profiling and self-confirming anxiety loops.
- A conversational response can show the *result* without exposing every private candidate. For a consequential recommendation or attempted action, give an adequate user-facing rationale citing authorized evidence, uncertainties and applicable permission, without claiming to reveal exact hidden model reasoning. Where the relevant reasons cannot be shared without violating another person's privacy, say so without disclosing their identity, private details or even a private record's existence when that itself is sensitive. Fail closed rather than acting on inaccessible evidence alone.
- Autonomous activity requires a real worker invocation with time/resource budgets and foreground priority; no fictional activity during shutdown. No hidden web access, external actions, DM delivery, or always-on claim is conferred by private reflection.

## UI: private journal, books, binders and pages

An optional personal journal/scratch-paper metaphor may project **authorized, selected** reflections to Sofía's own scoped workspace. A loose page can represent a candidate; an annotation a correction; a closed notebook an archived thread. This is a view of actual stored records, not a continuous thinking animation. No automatic page spawn for every token or continuous animation when the worker is idle/offline.

The public workbench only receives approved material after authorization and redaction. Audience isolation applies to object **existence, title, artwork, count, metadata, previews, interaction affordances, avatar gaze, notification badges and search indexes**, not merely page bodies. A locked folder showing that someone else's private folder exists may itself leak information: omit unauthorized objects entirely. Protect screen sharing, streaming, screenshots, exported scene state and renderer caches. The avatar may choose to present a permitted page but cannot open private material or grant permission by pointing at it. An absent renderer must not disable memory safety or text chat.

## Governance, retention and operator boundaries

Document who may configure private-reflection policy and which actions require independently authorized operational access. Record content-minimized access and sharing audit events, retention limits, delete/export capabilities subject to the applicable data subject's rights and constraints, encrypted storage where appropriate, backup restoration and revocation propagation into all derived notes, memory indexes and queued messages. A creator's administrative control of a machine is different from conversational permission to disclose others' private DMs. Do not offer misleading absolute secrecy guarantees against the host operator.

Separate safety audit from open-ended surveillance: default routine metrics and audits to metadata rather than exposing reflection text. A bounded, independent emergency stop and authorization checks apply to every proposed action regardless of private thought. Public explanations need not dump raw private reflections or fabricated chain-of-thought content, but must accurately identify relevant authorized facts, uncertainty and action outcome.

## Acceptance gates and negative tests

1. An actual scheduled/local event produces a sourced private candidate; a non-triggering period or process downtime produces no invented thoughts. Record observed run time; queued work does not count as completed reflection.
2. Candidate may be revisited and corrected, affect an appropriately scoped later idea/tone, or expire silently; no mandatory outreach or page creation. A serious reply remains answer-first and foreground latency remains acceptable.
3. Two private DMs and a shared channel: A's candidate and derived embeddings/summary never appear in B's content, attitude about A, recommendations, object existence/count/title, avatar behavior or logs accessible to B. Group-to-DM and spoofed actor failures reject access. Account linking requires proof and explicit grant.
4. An intentionally approved non-sensitive project idea can be shared with the named audience with provenance; sharing one derivative never silently exposes its source transcript. Revoke/erase and backup restore do not reinstate access, including generated scene caches and unsent outbox.
5. A private candidate cannot authorize Discord messages, change Constitution, grant tools, certify a physical touch, escalate urgency without verified evidence, or cause unsupervised external execution. Private reflections never bypass STOP or audit.
6. Consequential decision explanations remain useful and honest while protecting inaccessible sources. No false claim that Sofía was thinking while powered off, subjectively felt emotion, or possesses inaccessible private knowledge.
7. Mark contract, fixtures, actual live provider, actual Discord/renderer and supervised 24/7 evidence separately as passed/failed/not-run; no deployment or implementation claim from this document.
