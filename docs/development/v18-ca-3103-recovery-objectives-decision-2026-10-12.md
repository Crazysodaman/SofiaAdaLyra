# V18 CA-3103 | Owner-selected recovery objectives (Option B)

**Owner decision:** 2026-10-12 (America/Chicago). **Status:** approved engineering targets; not an achieved recovery SLA, proof of replication or permission to change any live host. Related tasks: CA-3103 (Phase 00), CA-3701, CA-5207, CA-6108 (Phase 03). Original task identifiers/requirements remain unchanged.

## Selected service-level targets

- **RPO target: 5 minutes (300 seconds).** For a covered failure, the most recent independently durable, verified recoverable canonical state should be no more than five minutes behind the last committed original event. A timestamped copy alone does not prove that all committed writes, source assets and correction/deletion evidence are present.
- **RTO target: 1 hour (3,600 seconds).** Target an operator-verified, safely fenced restoration of the sole writer within an hour of incident detection. This includes identifying source and standby, verifying keys and source identity, applying the independent erasure journal, checking archive/asset integrity, confirming old-writer exclusion, promoting a monotonic epoch, bringing up the authorized runtime and checking authenticated clients. A narrower helper measuring only restore start to verification must not be presented as the complete incident RTO.
- **Method:** frequent verified checkpoints / incremental replication to a genuinely separate recovery failure domain. The design must specify and test maximum interval *plus* transfer, acknowledgement and verification lag. Merely scheduling a copy every five minutes cannot guarantee five-minute RPO. Capture last-known independently acknowledged source-event sequence, age, error state, integrity digest and missing assets; alerts must make stale or incomplete coverage explicit.
- **Single-writer invariant:** Venus is the only live canonical `sofia.db` writer today. After a separately supervised, authenticated and independently fenced cutover, the Sofia Debian VM on Artemis is the only writer. Hephaestus is never a canonical replica or alternate writer. The independent Artemis host controller must not treat an in-guest lease, a service STOP or an ordinary Fleet receipt as sufficient old-writer fencing.
- **Recovery strategy:** verified encrypted full snapshots provide a recoverable base, complemented by approved incremental copies/replay mechanisms that preserve transaction order, schemas, independent memory-erasure evidence and associated media. No automatic failover, dual writable SQLite or model-authorized writer election. Unknown replication state requires operator review, not fabricated green health.
- **Retention distinction:** existing owner decision remains **two encrypted backup snapshots per approved destination**. A five-minute RPO is *not* a decision to rotate those two full snapshots every five minutes. Incremental journal/WAL/archive retention, long-term storage quota, encryption-key escrow and the independent/offsite location remain subject to separate source design and operator approval.
- **Failure handling:** loss of the selected backup destination, encryption key, independent deletion journal or verified replicator acknowledgement means the RPO target is unverified/degraded. Never advertise recovery as possible when a required authority or key is missing; never silently revert the authoritative lineage or resurrect erased memory.

## Engineering and acceptance gates

1. Prove a complete source-event-to-recovery-candidate path rather than just StatePlane rows, including legacy SQLite tables, assets, approvals, revoked entries, corrections, encrypted manifests and external erasure journal.
2. Demonstrate on independently staged data and both Windows/Linux restore paths: interruption, corrupt/incomplete candidate, stale epoch, concurrent old-writer restart, network outage, disk full, key unavailable and target loss; no split brain or memory resurrection.
3. Report **observed RPO** as the age of the newest independently verified recoverable canonical commit relative to the failure point, and **observed RTO** as elapsed time from incident detection to authorized usable service. Include sample counts, worst case, unknown cases and exact Git/host evidence. `RecoveryObjectives` helper predicates are policy checks, not proof of either measurement.
4. Hold the production release/deployment gate closed until a verified recovery rehearsal satisfies the chosen targets or the owner explicitly revises them. A CI simulation is not a live recovery receipt.

## Unresolved implementation/deployment questions

Exact storage destination and independent failure domain, incremental transport and acknowledgement, protected key custody, RPO/RTO instrumentation, monthly growth/cost limits and actual acceptance drills are still unapproved or unverified. Do not infer these from host names or two-snapshot retention. No live data, service, VM, network, credential, pointer or writer configuration is changed by this decision record.
