# CA-4906 | Media memory summarization and raw-source deletion preference

**Owner direction finalized 2026-10-12:** Temporarily capture only independently authorized media, summarize eligible experiences accurately into scoped durable memory, and then delete eligible disposable originals. The owner has approved the exact summary-success and summarization-failure deadlines, completed-artifact exceptions and the temporary storage cap, detailed below. This is design approval, not proof that deletion is implemented.

## Preferred policy shape

1. **Temporary captures rather than permanent recordings:** When Sofía is separately authorized to capture a screen observation, voice clip, photo or short video for a specific permitted purpose, the resulting raw bytes are **transient**. They must not become a permanent autobiography merely because a capture was technically possible. Recording remains subject to its own scope, purpose, principal/audience, capture consent, secure handling and stop controls.
2. **Evidence-grounded consolidation:** If retained knowledge is appropriate under existing memory consent/eligibility, create a **source-linked, privacy-scoped, accurate and revisable summary** with observed time, source type, confidence/uncertainty, principal/audience, any useful project reference and precise claims. Do not promote incidental sensitive content, credentials, third-party private material, recordings of protected applications or model-generated speculation into durable memory. An unauthorized observation should not be 'legitimized' by first summarizing it.
3. **Delete disposable raw bytes after verified consolidation:** Only after a successful authorized summary transaction and durable receipt should an eligible temporary raw capture be scheduled for deletion from **controlled active local media storage**. A failed summarizer, no approved memory, or failed receipt must never create a false success, accidental deletion of the only authorized original, or a permanent unsupervised accumulation of raw captures. The owner has now approved **24 hours after verified successful summary** as the deletion deadline for eligible disposable active-media bytes. If no eligible summary is committed, retry only within **24 hours of the original capture**, then delete by that deadline while logging the non-content failure. Fail closed on unsupported retention rather than defaulting to unlimited storage.
4. **Preserve separate classes of original artifacts:** User messages/canonical conversation, completed creative works, source files needed to re-evaluate LIFE/GOALS technical claims, explicit keeps, independent legal/audit records and approved backup/checkpoint originals are **not automatically disposable observation clips**. A text summary is not equivalent to verification of the original artifact or cryptographic source bytes. Such assets need their own visible keep/delete policy and may require fresh exact authorization.
5. **Deletion scope is honest:** A delete receipt must distinguish removal of an exact eligible active-media blob from encrypted backup generations, remote clients, provider-side Discord copies, exported files, immutable evidence, caches and derived memory. Honor tombstones/restore anti-resurrection where supported. Existing owner-selected two encrypted backup snapshots per destination still apply; they do not automatically erase individual historic media.
6. **Minimum privacy/accounting controls:** per-class allowed MIME/type and file size, encryption, quotas, deduplication by authorized scope, non-executable media parsing, license/source manifest, malware scanning as appropriate, and operator ability to inspect what was summarized, kept, failed or deleted without leaking raw private content into LLM prompts.
7. **Don't self-authorize:** Summary or deletion cannot grant screen/microphone capture, transfer to a shared audience, access to local private activity, or deletion of important original evidence. Existing SAFE/owner controls and device/game restrictions remain binding.

## Confirmed owner numerical policy

- **Retention success:** verified eligible summary at T1, raw disposable capture deletion due T1 + 24 hours.
- **Retention failure:** limited accurate/authorized summary retry from capture time T0 through T0 + 24 hours. At the 24-hour capture deadline, if no valid summary exists, purge exact raw media and record bounded non-content failure; do not invent memory or keep indefinitely.
- **Keep original creative work:** finished artifact originals survive until separately owner-authorized cleanup; only temporary drafts can expire under a reviewed draft policy.
- **Capture quota:** maximum **2 GiB (2,147,483,648 bytes) of temporary capture storage per host**. If capacity is full, refuse further capture, not delete protected originals to create space.
- **Separate capture authority:** retaining a summary does not grant microphone/screen/image capture, consent, sharing or SAFE permission.

## Implementation and acceptance

Current `src/sofia/creative/store.py` holds project/artifact metadata including original paths and verification links; this is not a tested summary→exact-media deletion worker. Existing `memory/deletion_ledger.py` is specifically for individually approved **memory candidates**, not blanket media storage deletion. A new proposal must not re-purpose that narrow erasure as if it already removes recordings or original files. Acceptance requires transactional/source-aware summary evidence, exact target deletion, expiry, crash/restart recovery, concurrent reader/writer fencing, principal/audience isolation, durable privacy manifest, duplicate/revocation checks, backup honesty and Windows/Linux storage tests.

**Status:** owner retention deadlines, draft/artifact exception and 2-GiB quota are decided. Media lifecycle enforcement, authenticated capture eligibility and actual source-to-sink deletion are **not** demonstrated or accepted. Do not run destructive cleanup or modify live permissions by recording this design.
