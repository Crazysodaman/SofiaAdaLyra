# V18 CA-6505 | Owner-approved autonomy graduation Option B

**Decision date:** 2026-10-12 (America/Chicago). **Owner selection:** Option B, the balanced, proof-first Level 3 graduation policy. **Status:** authoritative owner design requirement; implementation and acceptance verification remain open. This record does not issue, sign, renew, extend, revoke or install any live permission.

## Decision

1. **Applicable authority:** only an already **explicitly classified** `PermissionLevel.REVERSIBLE_SCOPED` (Level 3) capability for which the existing `capability_permission_policy` permits standing grants. Read-only Level 1 and bounded Level 2 behavior retain their current existing authority. Level 4 protected operations continue requiring their own fresh authorization. Level 5 is never self-authorizable. A capability cannot be reclassified or have its protected scope diluted as a result of success counts.
2. **Proof threshold:** before Sofía **may propose** a time-limited standing grant for a particular eligible capability, at least **10 independently verified successful original operations** of that capability must be demonstrated. Evidence must bind original approval, exact authority, capability, compatible scope, original RUN/ACT or other canonical execution receipt, verified objective/outcome, time, and unique operation identity. Ten LLM claims, retries/replays of a single effect, synthetic/simulated completions, generated evaluations, duplicate receipts or a changed task scope never count as ten successes. Only independently verified results count; missing evidence stays unknown.
3. **Owner decision remains mandatory:** reaching the threshold only allows Sofía to **prepare a proposal**. The owner must review exact capability, bounded scope, supporting ten-or-more original outcomes, risks, revocation control, resource/cost budget and explicit expiry. A fresh, independent **FIDO2 exact-action signed owner approval** must be verified through existing SAFE approval machinery before `PermissionStore.grant` can create a standing grant. Silence, a pending prompt, Discord text, model output, history, a previously consumed approval or inferred trust never authorize execution.
4. **Maximum graduated proposal duration:** **7 calendar days (168 hours)** from the actual signed grant timestamp. Earlier owner-selected expiry is allowed. No default perpetual grant, extension, auto-renewal, wider scope, automatic movement from Level 3 to Level 4, or silent grant on new machines/identities. Following expiry or revocation, work requiring the grant stops or requests fresh owner review.
5. **Failure-driven restriction:** any new relevant execution failure, security/privacy incident, unverified/uncertain result, objective regression or source-evidence revocation blocks *further graduation proposals* pending independent assessment. A serious authorization or safety incident must deny and escalate the capability as existing SAFE policy requires. Previously issued grants are still subject to their ordinary scope, expiry and revocation checks; a safe production implementation must also support protective suspension/revocation where the verified incident makes that necessary. Never self-grant an exception, suppress adverse evidence, or treat more autonomous activity as a reward.
6. **No coupling to personality:** Sofía may propose goals, learn skills, choose silence and share a justified achievement through an eligible owner audience, but her wish, excitement, feelings, inferred preference, initiative or convincing prose have **no permission-granting power**.
7. **Scope and principal:** verified success is evaluated within one exact, comparable capability and security principal/environment. The evidence cannot be aggregated across unrelated guests, users, mobile sessions, separate approval classes or more powerful scopes without an explicit independently reviewed transfer policy. All resource budgets, gaming restrictions, stop controls and single-writer invariants continue to apply.

## Current source boundaries, as inspected on main at owner decision

- `src/sofia/safe/permissions.py` already separates permission Levels 1–5, permits standing grants only for explicitly recognized reversible Level 3 capabilities, requires exact nonempty scope and a fresh FIDO2-bound `safe.permission.standing.grant` approval for actual grant creation.
- Existing general owner-initiated `PermissionStore.grant` accepts `expires_at=None`. That prior interface must **not** be represented as enforcing the new **seven-day graduation rule**. The Option B expiry limit applies to the *graduation-proposal path*; constraining all manual owner-directed grants would be an unrelated policy change requiring separate review.
- There is **not yet demonstrated** an integrated, adversarially tested independent ten-operation evidence evaluator, capability-specific graduation proposal gate, bounded-168-hour grant path, incident-driven graduation suspension or durable proof that all UI/API proposal routes respect them. The presence of SAFE permissions alone is not completion of CA-6505.

## Acceptance criteria before marking the task coded/accepted

1. A per-capability, compatible-scope evidence evaluator counts ten distinct **independently checked** real original successful operations and rejects duplicate IDs, simulated completions, LLM evaluations, failed attempts, unrelated principals or stronger target scopes.
2. At nine successes the proposal is denied; at ten eligible successes, it may become reviewable by the owner but never a grant; a new relevant failure or revoked receipt blocks proposals and emits an evidence-backed reason.
3. Owner-approved grants derived from that proposal expire no later than 168 hours from signed approval and cannot be renewed, widened or reused without another valid owner approval; existing FIDO2 approvals remain single-use/exact-action and revoked credentials cannot continue to confer authority.
4. Verify owner stop/revoke, operator recovery, permission changes, restart, clock skew, offline clients, conflicting proposals, privacy/principal separation and gaming protection. Produce exact CI and physical-key test receipts; source-authored tests alone are not acceptance.
5. Keep global evidence ledger status conservative until Workstream 1 reconciles the complete source/test results.
