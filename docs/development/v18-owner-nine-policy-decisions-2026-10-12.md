## Retention lifecycle ambiguity resolved for engineering

- **Success path:** raw capture at T0; verified eligible summary committed at T1; disposable raw blob due for deletion at T1 + 24h. Any protected original needed for independent verification stays under its separate access/retention policy.
- **Unsuccessful path:** original capture at T0; bounded reattempts only before T0 + 24h; if no eligible verified summary at that deadline, raw capture must be purged then (or fail closed, quarantine, record a deletion fault and alert authorized owner if purge cannot be proven). No fictitious memory gets promoted.
- **No summary permitted:** if an observation was not authorized, would breach privacy, or is ineligible for durable memory, do not preserve it under a claimed summary; remove temporary raw media according to the separate immediate privacy/short-lived failure policy, never use the full grace window as an excuse for unapproved storage.
- **Original data risk:** summary-first cleanup may destroy the original supporting evidence for future factual verification; keep an honest deletion receipt and mark summary as retained but **original no longer available**. A later claim requiring the original must abstain, re-observe with consent or rely on other valid evidence. Controlled retention and legal/audit exceptions require exact classification; they are not blanket unlimited media retention.
- **Protected system modes:** background retries never override game mode, manual STOP, transport/offline restrictions, privacy revocation or exhaustion of the daily background compute budget. Expiry obligations should not depend on successfully loading an LLM. No automatic canonical-memory candidate erasure beyond already approved exact-ID journal policy.
